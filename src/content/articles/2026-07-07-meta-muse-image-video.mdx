## TL;DR

Meta launched Muse Image in Meta AI with web search, self-correction, precise edits, and support for several reference images. Its output carries an invisible Content Seal. Muse Video was previewed but is not generally available yet.

## What's New

Muse Image is Meta Superintelligence Labs' first released image model. It can generate new pictures, edit selected details, combine several references, and use search or code when a request needs current facts or exact visual elements.

Meta says the model can revise its own draft when it notices a problem. Images made through the Meta AI app and meta.ai also receive Content Seal, an invisible provenance signal designed to survive common edits such as cropping, resizing, compression, and screenshots.

Muse Image is available through Meta AI, Instagram Stories in the United States, and WhatsApp in a limited set of countries. Muse Video, which includes generated audio, is still a preview and is promised for a later release.

## Why It Matters

Image generators are becoming editing tools rather than one-shot prompt boxes. The useful test is whether a model can keep the parts you like while changing one specific object, line of text, or layout detail.

Meta's social integration also deserves care. Only upload photos you own or have permission to use, and do not assume an invisible watermark makes a misleading image harmless or obvious to every viewer.

1. Open [Meta AI](https://www.meta.ai/) and start an image-generation chat.
2. Create a simple poster with a title, one object, and a two-color palette.

4. Upload a photo you own and request one narrow edit, such as replacing the background without changing the person.
