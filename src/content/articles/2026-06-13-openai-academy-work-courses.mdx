## TL;DR

OpenAI added three Academy courses for people trying to use AI at work: basics, repeatable workflows, and agent-assisted workflows. Treat them less like training content to collect and more like a 20-minute test: can one lesson improve a task you repeat every week?

## What's New

OpenAI introduced AI Foundations, Applied AI Foundations, and Agents and Workflows on OpenAI Academy. The sequence starts with prompting and output review, moves into reusable workflow design, and then covers directing agents with context, boundaries, and review.

The courses are aimed at workplace adoption, not model research. OpenAI says learners can receive completion certificates, and organizations can use the material for onboarding or broader AI fluency programs.

The practical promise is simple: a team should be able to move from one-off prompting to a shared way of doing recurring work with checkpoints and human review.

## Why It Matters

Most people do not need another list of prompts. They need a way to turn a useful AI trick into a repeatable habit without quietly lowering quality.

This is why the Agents and Workflows course is the interesting part. The everyday skill is not just asking an AI for an answer; it is defining inputs, outputs, boundaries, and review points so the work can be repeated safely.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="20 min" prerequisites={["A browser","An OpenAI Academy account or willingness to sign in"]}>

1. Open OpenAI Academy and sign in or join if prompted.
2. Open the Courses section and choose the course that matches your level: AI Foundations, Applied AI Foundations, or Agents and Workflows.
3. Pick one recurring task you actually do, such as meeting notes, weekly planning, or customer-response drafting.
4. Complete the first lesson or module, then write down one workflow rule you can reuse next time.
5. Try that rule on the real task once and keep it only if it saves time without lowering quality.
