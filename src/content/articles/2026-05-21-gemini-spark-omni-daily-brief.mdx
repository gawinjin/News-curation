## TL;DR

Google is turning Gemini from a chat app into a more active assistant. The new pieces are Daily Brief for morning context, Gemini Omni for video creation and editing, and Spark, a personal agent that can work across connected apps with user approval.

## What's New

Google says Gemini now serves more than 900 million monthly users, up from 400 million at last year's I/O. The app update brings a new design, richer responses, and Gemini Live woven more directly into the normal chat flow.

The two practical additions are Daily Brief and Spark. Daily Brief can gather updates from Gmail and Calendar after you opt in, then turn them into a prioritized morning digest. Spark is a cloud-based personal agent built on Gemini 3.5 and the Antigravity harness, designed to keep working in the background across Workspace tools.

Google also introduced Gemini Omni for video. It can use text, images, and video prompts to create or edit videos, including background changes, cinematic moves, templates, and avatar creation. Omni starts rolling out to paid Google AI subscribers.

## Why It Matters

The key change is permissioned background work. A chatbot answers when you ask. An agent watches, organizes, drafts, and reminds while you are away. That can save time, but it also means the setup choices matter more.

For normal users, the safe way to think about Spark and Daily Brief is not "turn everything on." Start with one connected app, one task, and one review habit. Agents are most useful when their job is narrow enough that you can spot mistakes.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="15 min" prerequisites={["A Google account", "Gemini app access", "One low-risk task to test"]}>

1. Open Gemini and check whether the new interface, Daily Brief, or connected-app settings are available in your account.
2. If Daily Brief is available, connect only the lowest-risk source first, such as Calendar rather than Gmail.
3. Ask Gemini to make a morning plan from your next three calendar events, then compare it with your actual calendar.
4. If Spark access appears later, give it a harmless task first, such as summarizing non-sensitive meeting notes into a draft checklist.
5. Before adding Gmail, Docs, or other connections, write down what the agent is allowed to do and what it must ask you before doing.

</PracticalGuide>
