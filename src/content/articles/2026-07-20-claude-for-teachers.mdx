## TL;DR

Verified US K-12 educators can get a free year of Claude if they register by June 30, 2027. The offer combines premium access with teaching tools for standards-linked lesson planning, differentiated materials, class-data analysis, and recurring tasks. Anthropic says the service does not use teacher inputs or outputs for model training.

## What's New

Claude for Teachers is available to verified adults employed by US K-12 schools or districts. The free year includes teaching-specific skills and a Learning Commons connection covering academic standards in all 50 states.

Teachers can use it to draft lessons, adapt materials for different readiness levels, analyze class data, and schedule repeated work such as reviewing exit tickets. The package also includes Claude Code, Cowork, and integrations with several education tools.

Anthropic says teacher inputs and outputs are excluded from model training and its K-12 terms are designed around FERPA requirements. This offer is for educators rather than student accounts; a separate product for schools and districts is planned.

## Why It Matters

The useful part is not simply free access to a chatbot. Teachers get a quicker way to turn a topic and state standard into a draft lesson or assessment, then adjust it for different learners. That can reduce preparation time while leaving the educator responsible for reviewing the result.

The privacy terms matter because classroom work can involve sensitive information. For a first test, use a lesson topic and standard without names, grades, attendance records, or other identifiable student data.
