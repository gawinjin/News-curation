## TL;DR

OpenAI's Dots are always-on assistants that can pursue goals from an isolated cloud computer. Access is rolling out to eligible paid accounts. Start with a reminder that uses no plugins or sensitive information, then inspect its activity, pause it, and set approval rules before trusting it with broader work.

## What's New

Dots run on GPT-6 Astra and can keep several projects moving at once from an isolated cloud computer. They report progress, ask questions, and carry context across supported channels.

OpenAI says a dot can connect to more than 4,000 apps through plugins. People can talk with it through ChatGPT on desktop, web, and mobile, plus Slack and Teams; text messaging is planned. Users choose which apps it can access, while Custom Rules can allow, block, or require approval for actions. Proactive background research remains read-only, and some sensitive actions stay with the user.

The gradual rollout starts with eligible Pro and Business Premium accounts, with one dot included. Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare workspaces need an administrator to enable the beta. A dot must currently be created in a desktop browser or the desktop app, not on mobile.

## Why It Matters

A persistent assistant could keep reminders and longer-running projects moving while you are away. The safest first test is deliberately small: use non-sensitive information, avoid plugins, confirm the scheduled task appears in the activity views, and try the pause control before assigning broader work.

Rules and approvals reduce risk; they do not guarantee correct results, so review consequential work. Before connecting personal apps, check Data Controls. Disconnecting a plugin prevents future access but does not erase information already retained; deleting the dot removes its conversations, memories, and scheduled tasks, subject to OpenAI's stated retention and legal exceptions.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="10 min" prerequisites={["Age 18 or older and Dots access on an eligible Pro, Business Premium, or administrator-enabled workspace account","ChatGPT in a desktop browser or the desktop app","Non-sensitive test information"]}>

1. Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) on desktop, sign in, and select Create your dot. Complete onboarding and give it a name. If the option is absent, your account has not received the gradual rollout; stop and check later.
2. Open the dot profile, then Customize > Custom rules. Add: ‘Ask me before sending, purchasing, deleting, installing, or changing anything outside this chat.’ Choose Ask before taking action and save it.
3. Send: ‘Five minutes from now, remind me to review tomorrow’s top priority. Do not use connected apps or contact anyone.’ Confirm the dot says it scheduled the reminder.
4. Open the dot profile > Scheduled and confirm the reminder and run time. After it runs, check that the update appears in the dot conversation.
5. Review In progress, Scheduled, and Completed. Then open the profile menu, choose Pause, confirm the Paused label, and resume it. Before using plugins, review Data Controls; remember that disconnecting a plugin stops future access but deleting the dot is what removes its conversations, memories, and schedules.

</PracticalGuide>
