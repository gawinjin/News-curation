## TL;DR

Google has announced Gemini 3.8 Live and an Extended Thinking version for more demanding spoken tasks. The latter is starting to reach Gemini Live. Try a short speaking rehearsal and change your request mid-answer, but remember: the rollout does not guarantee that your account already has the new model.

## What's New

The September 15 announcement separates two jobs. Gemini 3.8 Live targets efficient back-and-forth conversation; Extended Thinking targets problems that need more reasoning. Google says the latter can work through a task while continuing to speak, rather than leaving the conversation silent until it finishes.

The consumer destinations differ: Google lists Search Live for the standard model and Gemini Live for Extended Thinking. Developer access is also rolling out through Google's API and AI Studio. This article's exercise uses the mobile app, not a developer key or installation command.

Google's Android help page explains how to start Live, interrupt a response, and review a transcript. Those controls already exist; they are not evidence that you have received 3.8. Google explicitly warns that new Live features arrive gradually.

## Why It Matters

An interview rehearsal or a short introduction is easier to refine when you can interrupt an answer that is heading in the wrong direction. Our suggested test focuses on that useful behavior, not on whether a voice sounds impressive. Ask for one change and see whether the next response actually reflects it.

Keep the first attempt low-stakes. Use invented details, avoid recording other people, and compare the transcript with what you remember saying. A fluent spoken answer still needs checking.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="10 min" prerequisites={["An Android phone or tablet with the Gemini app and an up-to-date Google app","An eligible signed-in Google account with Gemini Live available","Microphone access and a quiet place; use fictional details"]}>

1. Open the Gemini app on Android, tap **Live** at the bottom, and follow the microphone prompts. Check [Google's access requirements](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15274899?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en) if Live is missing; do not buy a subscription just to follow this test.
2. Say: "Help me rehearse a short introduction for a community event. Ask one question at a time, then give one specific improvement." Use an invented name and event.
3. Answer the first question. While Gemini replies, interrupt with: "Make it less formal, and keep the introduction under thirty seconds." Check whether it follows the changed request instead of finishing the old answer.
4. Rehearse once more. Judge the result against three checks: it asked one question at a time, accepted your correction, and gave advice you could actually use.
5. Tap **End** and inspect the transcript. Note any missed words or invented details. This tests the Live experience available to you, not which model your account is running.

</PracticalGuide>
