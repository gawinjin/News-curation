## TL;DR

OpenAI previewed GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna. The headline is stronger agentic work, coding, science, and cyber-defense capability, but access begins with a limited trusted-partner preview before broader ChatGPT, Codex, and API rollout.

## What's New

GPT-5.6 introduces a three-tier family. Sol is the flagship model, Terra is the balanced everyday option, and Luna is positioned as the faster, lower-cost option.

OpenAI says GPT-5.6 adds a new max reasoning effort for deeper work and an ultra mode that can use subagents for complex tasks. The announcement points to gains in coding, biology workflows, and cybersecurity.

The release is deliberately staged. OpenAI says the models start with trusted partners, with broader access planned for ChatGPT, Codex, and the API in the coming weeks.

## Why It Matters

For normal users, the important part is not the benchmark table. It is the product direction: AI tools are becoming more capable at multi-step work, but the most powerful versions are arriving with more access controls, pricing tiers, and safety checks.

If you use ChatGPT or Codex for serious work, prepare one repeatable task now. That gives you a clean way to tell whether GPT-5.6 actually helps your work when it reaches your account.
