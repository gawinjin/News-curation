## TL;DR

Anthropic released Claude Science in beta. It is not just Claude with a lab coat: it is a scientific workbench with domain tools, local or remote compute, auditable artifacts, and an application window for AI for Science projects.

## What's New

Claude Science is available in beta for Claude Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise users. It runs on macOS or Linux, and can also work with remote machines through SSH or HPC login nodes.

The app comes with scientific skills and connectors for fields such as genomics, single-cell analysis, proteomics, structural biology, and cheminformatics. Anthropic says reviewer agents check citations, calculations, and figures.

Anthropic is also supporting up to 50 AI for Science projects with credits, with applications open through July 15, 2026.

## Why It Matters

Research work is full of slow handoffs: papers, databases, notebooks, figures, compute jobs, and reviewer comments. Claude Science tries to put more of that work in one auditable session.

That does not remove the need for scientific judgment. It changes where the judgment goes: users need to inspect the sources, code, environment, and generated artifacts instead of treating the AI output as a final answer.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="20 min" prerequisites={["Claude Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise","A non-sensitive sample paper, dataset, or figure"]}>

1. Open the Claude Science page and confirm whether the beta is available for your plan and operating system.
2. Use a public paper or sample dataset first; do not start with unpublished or patient-sensitive material.
3. Ask Claude Science for a literature-summary or figure-review task with citations and code history visible.
4. Check every citation, number, and generated figure against the source before reusing it.
5. If you are in an academic or nonprofit lab, review the AI for Science project application before the July 15 deadline.
