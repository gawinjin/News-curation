## TL;DR

ChatGPT ads are now live in five more countries for eligible adult Free and Go users. Ads sit below answers and carry a Sponsored label. You can limit personalization, erase ad data, inspect or report a placement, or choose an ad-free option with reduced Free-tier usage.

## What's New

OpenAI launched ChatGPT ads in the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea on August 11, after earlier pilots in the United States, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. The original test covered logged-in adults on Free and Go accounts; Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Education accounts remain ad-free.

Sponsored placements appear below ChatGPT's response, with a label and visual separation from the answer. OpenAI says advertisers cannot change the response. Free users who want no ads can accept a lower daily message allowance, while Plus and Pro also offer an ad-free experience.

Ad selection may draw on the current topic, past chats, and earlier ad interactions. OpenAI says advertisers receive combined performance totals such as views and clicks, not individual chats, memories, histories, or personal details. Ads are also kept away from accounts identified as under 18 and conversations about health, mental health, or politics.

## Why It Matters

An ad beside a useful answer can feel like part of the recommendation even when it is not. The Sponsored label and separate placement give you a visible boundary, while Ad Controls let you see why a placement appeared, reduce personalization, clear ad data, or report something unsafe or misleading.

Those controls do not make an advertised claim true. Check important product details with an independent source before spending money, and remember that an advertiser can see anything you deliberately send through its messaging option. Temporary Chat provides a quick way to ask a question without an ad appearing.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="10 min" prerequisites={["A logged-in adult ChatGPT Free or Go account","A supported country where the ad test is active","A browser or the latest ChatGPT iOS app"]}>

1. Open your ChatGPT profile menu, choose Settings, and look for Ad Controls. If it is absent after updating the app, stop: your account may not be eligible yet.
2. Open Ad Controls and review Personalize ads, Past chats and memory, and History and Topics. Turn off either personalization switch you do not want used for ad relevance.
3. Choose Delete ads data if you want to clear ad history and topics. Confirm the deletion, remembering that this does not delete chats and system removal can take up to 30 days.
4. Start a non-sensitive comparison of two everyday products. If an ad appears, confirm that it is below the answer, visually separated, and labeled Sponsored.
5. Use the ad's three-dot menu to open About this ad. Hide an irrelevant ad or report a misleading or unsafe one, and independently check product claims before buying.
6. Repeat the comparison in Temporary Chat and confirm that no ad appears. Do not send private information to an advertiser; it can see messages you deliberately send to it.
