## TL;DR

Gemini 3.6 Flash is Google's new general-purpose fast model for coding, documents, images, audio, and video. It is available in the Gemini app and developer tools now. Google also released a cheaper Flash-Lite model, while the cyber version remains restricted to governments and trusted partners.

## What's New

Google says Gemini 3.6 Flash improves coding, knowledge work, computer use, and multimodal understanding over 3.5 Flash. Its model card lists a one-million-token input window and support for text, images, video, audio, and PDFs.

Google reports 17% fewer output tokens than 3.5 Flash on one independent index. API input pricing stays at $1.50 per million tokens, while output pricing falls from $9 to $7.50 per million tokens.

The companion 3.5 Flash-Lite targets high-volume, lower-cost work. Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is different: it is planned only for governments and trusted partners through a limited CodeMender pilot, so normal users should not expect it in the Gemini app.

## Why It Matters

Flash models are often the ones people use for ordinary work because they respond quickly. Better document and chart handling with less verbosity can matter more day to day than a larger model that takes longer to answer.

Google's benchmarks are useful signals, not proof for your own files. A ten-minute document test will tell you more about missed facts, unwanted edits, and usable output than a leaderboard.
