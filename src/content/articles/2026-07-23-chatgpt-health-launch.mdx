## TL;DR

ChatGPT Health is rolling out to U.S. users age 18 and older on web and iOS. It can read connected medical records and Apple Health data with permission, but it is not a diagnosis service. Start without connecting sensitive data, check every important detail, and understand what disconnection does not delete.

## What's New

Health now appears as a ChatGPT area for eligible Free, Go, Plus, and Pro users. People can ask health questions without connecting anything, or optionally connect Apple Health, supported U.S. medical records, One Medical, and Function Health.

OpenAI says connected records, Apple Health data, and conversations that use them are not used to train its foundation models or target ads. ChatGPT asks before using connected health information by default, though users can grant ongoing permission.

Disconnecting a source removes synced data from OpenAI's systems within 30 days. Health information already written into conversation history remains until those chats are deleted. OpenAI also warns that connected records can be incomplete or outdated and that ChatGPT can make mistakes.

## Why It Matters

Health data is useful precisely because it is sensitive and scattered. Bringing records into one assistant can make appointment preparation and trend review easier, but it also creates a new place where users must manage permissions, memory, and retained conversations.

The safest first test does not require uploading a complete medical history. Use a low-risk question, inspect the controls, and treat the answer as preparation for a clinician rather than medical authority.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="15 min" prerequisites={["A U.S. ChatGPT account for someone age 18 or older","ChatGPT on the web or iOS","A low-risk health question you can verify"]}>

1. Open ChatGPT and look for Health in the sidebar or More menu; stop if the gradual rollout has not reached your account.
2. Open Health and read the connection and permission screens before linking any account.
3. Start without a connection: ask it to turn a non-urgent appointment topic into five questions for your clinician.
4. Check the questions against your own records and remove anything incorrect, outdated, or too sensitive to retain.
5. If you choose to connect data, grant only the source you need, review conditions and medications after syncing, and keep permission prompts enabled.
6. After testing, review Health > Accounts and your chat history separately; disconnecting a source does not delete details already copied into chats.

</PracticalGuide>
