## TL;DR

OpenAI has added GPT-6 Sol and Luna to ChatGPT Work and Codex, but not ordinary Chat. It presents both as faster, less expensive alternatives to its top-tier Astra model. Eligible users can compare them now, though gradual rollout and workspace settings may delay access.

## What's New

OpenAI positions Sol and Luna as faster, lower-cost ways to use advances introduced with GPT-6 Astra, which remains its highest-capability option. The company also says both models use a clearer, less verbose communication style derived from Astra.

In an internal test based on conversations where users had flagged factual errors, OpenAI says Sol made about half as many mistakes as GPT-5.6 Sol. OpenAI cautions that these cases were harder than typical use.

Sol and Luna are rolling out in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu users. Luna is also available to Free and Go users in the desktop app, but neither model is available in ordinary Chat yet. For developers, OpenAI lists separate API rates per million tokens: Sol costs $2 for input and $10 for output, while Luna costs $0.10 and $0.50 respectively. Those are API charges, not ChatGPT subscription prices.

## Why It Matters

For everyday work, the useful question is not which model wins in general, but which one handles your repeatable task accurately and clearly. A controlled comparison using the same prompt and reasoning level can reveal whether Sol or Luna is the better fit for that job.

Treat the guide below as a small personal test, not a broad model benchmark. If the models are missing from your Work picker, rollout timing or workspace controls may be the reason.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="15 min" prerequisites={["A browser or the latest ChatGPT desktop app","A signed-in Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Edu account with Work enabled","GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna visible in the Work model picker"]}>

1. Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com), sign in, and select Work. In the desktop app, select ChatGPT from the top-left menu, then choose Work at the top of the page. Stop if Work is unavailable; managed-workspace access may depend on admin settings.
2. Start a new Work chat, open the model picker, select GPT-6 Luna, and choose a reasoning level. Paste: 'Fictional event budget: venue $1,250; catering for 37 people at $28 each; transport $360; contingency 10% of the subtotal. Show the arithmetic, give the final total, and summarise it in three bullets.' Record the answer.
3. Start a fresh Work chat, select GPT-6 Sol, use the same reasoning level if both model menus offer it, and paste the identical prompt. Record the answer without showing Sol Luna's result.
4. Check both answers independently: catering is $1,036, the subtotal is $2,646, contingency is $264.60, and the final total is $2,910.60. Mark any arithmetic error, missing working, or unsupported change to the invented inputs.
5. Compare correctness, clarity, and time to the first useful answer. Keep the model that performed better on this exact task. If either model is absent, do not infer its performance; wait for rollout or ask your workspace admin.
