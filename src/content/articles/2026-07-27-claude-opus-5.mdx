## TL;DR

Claude Opus 5 is now the default model for Claude Max and Anthropic's strongest option for Pro subscribers. Anthropic says it is better at checking its own work and following constraints through multiple revisions. A short document-editing test can help you judge those claims against work you already understand.

## What's New

Anthropic has released Claude Opus 5 across its products. Max users get it as their default model, while Pro subscribers can select it as their most capable option. The company highlights gains in professional tasks, research, computer use, visual output, verification, and careful iteration.

For developers, the base API price stays at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens, the same as Opus 4.8. An optional Fast mode runs about 2.5 times faster at twice the base rate. Anthropic also reports that Opus 5 more than doubled Opus 4.8's score on Frontier-Bench v0.1 while lowering the cost of completing each task.

Anthropic says the model does not extend risky dual-use capabilities and still trails Mythos 5 in biology and offensive cybersecurity. Its launch materials include a system card with the company's capability and safety evaluations.

## Why It Matters

The useful question is not whether Opus 5 tops a benchmark. It is whether the model catches unsupported claims, preserves requirements during rewrites, and improves a draft without introducing new errors. Those behaviors matter when you use Claude for reports, proposals, research notes, or other work that needs several careful passes.

Anthropic recommends stating the desired response length and adjusting effort when balancing quality, speed, and cost. You can test the practical difference with a document you know well, then compare the result with another available model. Keep sensitive material out of the test and check every important claim yourself.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="15–20 min" prerequisites={["A Claude Pro or Max account","A non-sensitive 300–800 word document or familiar topic you can independently check"]}>

1. Open Claude, start a new chat, click the model name beside the send button, choose More models if needed, and select Opus 5. Stop if it is absent; your plan or organization may not enable it.
2. Paste a non-sensitive text you know well and ask: Review this text, identify the three most important issues, explain each plainly, and propose a revision without inventing missing facts.
3. Ask Opus 5 to check every claim against your original text, list uncertainties, and correct unsupported statements. Verify the changes yourself.
4. Ask it to shorten the revision by 30% while preserving every date, number, and named requirement. Compare both versions and confirm those details survived.
5. Optionally repeat the same task with another available model and compare factual accuracy, useful corrections, and constraint-following. Do not use either result for consequential decisions without your own review.

</PracticalGuide>
