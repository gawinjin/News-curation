## TL;DR

Before forwarding an AI-written message, read the whole draft, check every important claim, and rewrite it for the person receiving it. That small pause turns machine output into an answer you understand and can stand behind.

## What's New

Simon Willison highlights Niklas Gruhn's term for a person who passes along AI-generated material without checking it first. Gruhn argues that this shifts the work to the recipient, who must interpret and verify a response they did not ask the AI to produce.

Their alternative is simple: understand the draft, validate it, then communicate the substance in your own words. OpenAI gives similar advice, warning that ChatGPT can sound confident while being wrong and recommending independent checks for important claims, quotations, data, technical details, and references.

## Why It Matters

AI can produce a polished email, summary, or recommendation quickly, but polish does not prove that the message is correct or suited to its recipient. Forwarding it untouched can leave someone else to find the mistakes and decide what the answer was meant to say.

Rewriting after verification adds the part the AI cannot supply for you: responsibility for the message. It also prepares you to answer follow-up questions without searching through the original chat.

<PracticalGuide timeToTry="10 min" prerequisites={["A non-sensitive AI-generated draft","The original question and intended recipient","Independent sources for any important factual claims"]}>

1. Write the recipient's actual question in one sentence, then read the entire AI draft. Mark every claim or term you cannot explain yourself; if any remain unclear, do not forward the draft.
2. Open reliable source material and independently confirm any claim that could change the recipient's decision. Remove unsupported claims, and stop if a consequential point remains uncertain.
3. Ask whether you could answer a follow-up about each remaining claim without reopening the AI transcript. Research or omit anything you cannot defend.
4. Close the AI output and rewrite the answer for this specific person. Keep only the relevant conclusion, evidence links, and uncertainty; the message should stand alone.
5. Before sending, confirm the message contains no private material and that you can stand behind it as your response. If either check fails, ask for clarification or send nothing.

</PracticalGuide>
